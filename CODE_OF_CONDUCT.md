# Community code of conduct

Campus Wall welcomes respectful discussion, constructive feedback and contributions from people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Focus criticism on code, behavior and ideas, not people.
- Keep examples, screenshots and logs free of real student data, credentials, private addresses and other identifying information.
- Explain disagreement with evidence and leave room for others to revise their view.
- Respect the public-mirror boundary: a contribution never authorizes access to any production service.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or repeated personal attacks.
- Publishing personal data, private infrastructure details, credentials or non-public content.
- Posting unredacted security vulnerabilities in issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Reporting

For a conduct concern, request a private maintainer contact without posting sensitive details. For technical security concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation when needed to protect people, privacy or the public mirror.
